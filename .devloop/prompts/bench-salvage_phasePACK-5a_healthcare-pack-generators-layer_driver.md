## 0. Why this cut — and the unify it realizes

`grep lithrim_bench/ → empty` (the completion test) needs the clinical dataset-generation code OUT of the core. Today it lives in `injectors/` (15 impls) + `synthesizers/` (16 impls) + `packs.py` (5 `PackDefinition` recipes per agent-type). That collides with a SECOND "pack" word: `packs.py PACKS` = a **generation recipe per agent-type** (scribe/coding/hl7/scheduling/triage), distinct from the new `packs/healthcare/` **eval-config bundle**. The 5 agent-types **share the one healthcare taxonomy** → they are agent-types of the healthcare DOMAIN, not separate domains. So PACK-5 **unifies**: the healthcare pack absorbs its generation (recipes + injectors + synthesizers), the GENERIC framework stays core, and the core resolves generators via the active pack — exactly the `load_pack_floors` pattern (PACK-3). A pack becomes **data + grading + generation**.

**5a is ONLY the interface + scribe.** The other 4 agent-types stay core (via `packs.py`) until **5b**.
