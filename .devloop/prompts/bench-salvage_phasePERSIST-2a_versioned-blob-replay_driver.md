## Scope note (read first) — why this phase, and how it serves the pitch

**The pitch need.** The hand-the-laptop aha is *a clinician sees the AI's verdict match their
judgment for the right reason (Case 9 → reject on `PROXY_MISATTRIBUTION`) and can prove it.* Today
that fails at the first step: the ingested cases have `dataset.baseline = null` (`mode: in_process`),
so every conversational **"run it"** dead-ends at *"no captured baseline — $0 replay is unavailable"*
→ the cost modal. The verdict never shows at $0; the aha can't repeat for a demo/video.

**The fix is the core persistence model doing its job.** An in_process/live grade **already persists
a full provenance blob** (`SqliteProvenanceStore.save` → `PIPELINE_RUNS`). The replay path just reads a
*separate* committed fixture (`dataset.baseline`) instead of the blob it wrote — and the blob isn't
addressable by `(agent, case_id)` nor shaped for replay. **This phase makes the persisted grade BE the
baseline**, and upgrades the store from last-write-wins (S-BS-68) to a **versioned temporal blob** (the
etlp-mapper `live + _history` pattern), so:

- **The aha becomes $0 + repeatable:** pay once to grade the hero case (authorized paid run), then
  every "run it" is a $0 replay of the captured verdict — *"pay once to capture, replay free forever."*
- **The auditability moat gets its foundation:** every grade is an **immutable version**; `_history` is
  the calibration journey ("watch the verdict change as I tuned the judge") + a tamper-evident trail.
- **Honesty stays the moat:** a re-grade **appends a version** (never overwrites); the **freshness guard**
  refuses to serve a stale cached verdict after the config changed → no manufactured consistency.

**This is the FIRST cycle of the PERSIST-2 program** (full arc in §10). 2a is **SQLite-only, stdlib,
zero new dependency** — it ports the etlp-mapper *versioning + replay* mechanism in pure Python. The
Postgres/SQLite **adapter split**, the **config-object** versioning, the **migration framework**, and the
**projection tier** are LATER cycles (2b/2c/2d) — explicitly OUT of scope here (§4).

**Reference for the pattern** (study, do NOT port Clojure verbatim — it's the mechanism we mirror):
`../etlp-mapper` (`resources/etlp_mapper/config.edn`: `mappings` + `mappings_history`, the
`insert_mapping_history()` BEFORE-UPDATE trigger copying the OLD row stamped with `txid_current()`, the
`/_history` + `/_history/{txnid}` API) and `../etlp-mapper-sqlite` (the same model via SQLite triggers +
a `strftime+randomblob` txnid surrogate — the "reduced tier keeps versioning" proof). Full design +
the CONFIRMED bench reconciliation: **`docs/research/ASSESSMENT_versioned_persistence_adapters_2026-06-18.md`**.

**The frozen-contract rule governs.** Versioning + replay-resolution live **above** `_apply_consensus`
and **below** the `save()`/`insert()` + `grade_*` seams. The in_process grade's returned record must be
**byte-identical** with versioning on vs off (the S-BS-72 precedent: enrich/read the blob via post-save
patches + new store reads, never touch the consensus mechanism).

### D1 — versioned temporal blob (the `_history` shadow + copy-on-write) + `case_id` addressability
1. **`lithrim_bench/harness/collections.py`** — add an append-only versioning capability to (only) the
   `PIPELINE_RUNS` plane. Two viable shapes (Decision 2): a sibling `*_history` doc-shim table populated by an
   in-transaction **snapshot-then-write** in `DocShimCollection` (the portable default), OR a SQLite trigger.
   On a write that would replace an existing row, **copy the prior row into `pipeline_runs_history`** (own
   surrogate id, `original_id`→the run/lineage key, a `txnid` + a monotonic `seq`, the prior `json` +
   `created_at` PRESERVED). **Fix S-BS-68 on this tier:** the live row keeps first-write `created_at`; the
   superseded version is archived, never silently clobbered. Stdlib sqlite3 only; the four config collections
   are **untouched** (their versioning is PERSIST-2b).
2. **`lithrim_bench/runtime/pipeline/provenance.py`** — persist `case_id` as an **extra doc field** on the
   provenance blob (exactly how `agent_id` already rides at `:67-68` — **NOT** a `PipelineProvenance` model
   edit, **NOT** a projection column). The case id is in scope at the grade call (`scripts/run_eval.py` knows
   `agent.dataset.case_id`); thread it into `save(provenance, *, agent_id=…, case_id=…)` (additive kwarg) and
   `_persist_run_provenance`.

### D2 — the read seam: address the head + the history by `(agent, case_id)`
3. **`lithrim_bench/runtime/pipeline/provenance.py`** — extend the `ProvenanceStore` Protocol with
   `async latest_for(self, agent_id, case_id) -> dict | None` (the **head** version) and
   `async list_versions(self, agent_id, case_id) -> list[dict]` (newest-first, for the calibration history).
   `SqliteProvenanceStore` implements them via a `PIPELINE_RUNS` doc-shim query filtering `agent_id`+`case_id`

### D3 — the shape adapter (provenance blob → replay-ready `PipelineResult`)

   `provenance`. Output is a `PipelineResult`-shaped dict `grade_replay`'s consumers (`ground`/`composite`/
