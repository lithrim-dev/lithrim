## 0. Why this cycle exists (one paragraph)

WS-0..WS-2 built the live-grading walking skeleton: grade over `:8002`
(`harness/grade.py`), a SQLite config plane + ontology (`harness/{config,ontology}.py`),
and domain-agnostic config injection (WS-2 bench-side). `harness/grounding.py`
implements **one direction** of "grounding flips verdicts" — the S-BS-7
**suppress** half: a deterministic presence-check disproves a confident-but-wrong
judge finding (the `MEDICATION_NOT_IN_TRANSCRIPT` false positive) and removes it.
**WS-3 adds the other, authoritative direction — the structural floor:** the
ability to *author* a deterministic structural validator (a bench-accepted JUTE
mapping on `:3031`) and run it as a grounding contract that can flip a confident
**PASS → BLOCK** — catching the spec-violating artifact the council certified
(the paper's HL7 0/28 finding). The spike (`spike/verification-toolbox`) proved
the authoring works (DSPy generate→test→refine, bench-gated; 0/3 raw copilot →
accept; cross-domain; the mutation/joint gate measures oracle completeness). This
cycle **promotes the spike's core into `main` as an isolated, optional module and
wires its `VerificationContract` into `harness/grounding`.**
