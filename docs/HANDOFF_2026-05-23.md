## The open framing decision (the one thing this session left open)

After everything, the honest read is in PAPER_OUTLINE's 2026-05-23 scope-change note: **the worst-of recovery is unambiguously demonstrated only on HL7. The 4-pack sweep is baseline characterization.** That's not a thesis failure — it's the apparatus matching the claim more tightly than the original outline implied (PAPER_OUTLINE §11 always said v1 is "method+benchmark+protocol, not we-beat-baseline").

**Three reframe options, my recommendation is 1+2 together:**

1. **Reframe to honest scope** (cheap, doc-only): §1/§2/§9 layered around HL7 + judge-characterization. Already partially done — the 2026-05-23 scope-change note carries the new framing; §1/§2/§9 themselves are preserved for audit trail. The drafts (`docs/paper_draft/*.md`) are already test-split + corrected; minimal additional editing.
2. **Sharpen the headline with one small experiment**: measure council *confidence* on the 28 HL7 defects the council misses, so the headline becomes **"the council silently and confidently certifies spec-violating clinical artifacts"** — not just "validator catches what judge missed." We already have the data in council output (judge `confidence` / certainty signals are in the per-judge response). One small script, ~1 day, ~$few of LLM (HL7 cases are short). This turns the HL7 result from "true but obvious" into "alarming, non-obvious, paper-worthy."
3. **Bigger pivot** — make the *decomposition framework* (decision-stable / attribution-unstable / structural-blind-spot) the contribution, with worst-of as one application of it. Most general, biggest rewrite. **v2 paper, not v1.**
