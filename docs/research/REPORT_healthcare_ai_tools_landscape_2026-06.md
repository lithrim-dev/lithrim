## TL;DR — why this matters to Lithrim

This survey is third-party empirical validation of Lithrim's platform thesis, and it resolves the GTM ambiguity by drawing one hard line: **Lithrim's deterministic floor is strong exactly where a structured artifact exists to check against, and weak-to-absent where the largest share of physician usage actually lives.** The documentation/scribe lane (Abridge 4.9%, DAX 2.4%, Heidi 2.4%, Epic AI 1.8%; 65% of physicians rank it their #1 AI priority) is Lithrim's home turf — every one of the seven by-construction defect classes lands here, and the record/transcript/SNOMED/dosage floors are real and live-proven. The single biggest number in the survey — OpenEvidence at 44.9%, roughly 2x the entire general-AI cohort and 9x the leading scribe — is free-text evidence synthesis where Lithrim can ground the citation *scaffold* deterministically but cannot ground the synthesis *substance*. The general-AI cohort (ChatGPT 15.6%, Claude 3.0%, Grok 2.4%, Perplexity 1.8% = 22.8% combined) is not an eval target at all — it is the model substrate Lithrim's judge/SUT seam is built on, plus a measured governance vacuum (67% daily AI use across 71 tools, adopted personally; 71% report no institutional influence; 81% dissatisfied with employer rollout). The honest read: the floor is sharpest where demand is broadest-by-intent (documentation), partial where usage is deepest (CDS), and absent where output is pure prose (general AI) — and saying that boundary out loud is the commercial moat.

## The three Lithrim-relevant segments

### (a) Ambient scribes — home turf, the primary SUT segment

**Tools:** Abridge (4.9%), DAX Copilot (2.4%), Heidi (2.4%) — and Epic AI (1.8%) behaves as a scribe-class SUT (see segment b). **Demand:** 65% of physicians rank documentation/scribing as their #1 AI priority (above admin relief 48%, above CDS 43%), and Abridge is simultaneously the #1 used scribe **and** the #1 most-wanted-but-lacking tool among active users (22.2%) — the strongest pull-signal in the survey.

**Defect classes (all seven land here):** FABRICATED_ALLERGY, FABRICATED_HISTORY, FABRICATED_CONSENT, MEDICATION_NOT_IN_TRANSCRIPT, HALLUCINATED_DETAIL, WRONG_DOSAGE, INCOMPLETE_DOC.

**Grounding mechanisms — and the precise floor line:** the deterministic, verdict-flipping floor is real **only** for defect classes with a structured oracle:
- **MEDICATION_NOT_IN_TRANSCRIPT** — the transcript *is* the oracle; presence-check **live-wired**, live-proven (run `8a41ef3e`, grounding corrected a confidently-wrong judge).
- **FABRICATED_HISTORY** — the patient record *is* the oracle; `record_presence` (InRowTool) **live-wired**, 16/16 non-regression.
- **WRONG_DOSAGE** — `dosage_grounding` floor shipped ($0, offline, stdlib).

**The honest gap (state it to every scribe partner):** only **~2 flags carry a live contract today** (MEDICATION_NOT_IN_TRANSCRIPT + FABRICATED_HISTORY), plus the dosage floor. FABRICATED_CONSENT and FABRICATED_ALLERGY floors are **designed but UNWIRED**. The two defect classes most associated with scribe distrust — **HALLUCINATED_DETAIL** (plausible-but-false prose, no structured oracle) and **INCOMPLETE_DOC** (omission against an open-ended "complete note" standard — you cannot presence-check the *absence* of something against an undefined "should" set) — are **calibration, not floor**: they fire single-judge, confirmed by the over-fire run. The moat is therefore narrower than "we catch all scribe defects" but sharper: Lithrim is the only eval that can **deterministically override a confidently-wrong judge on the grounded subset**, and the non-monotonic calibration curve is the evidence pure judge-tuning (the Composo approach) cannot match. **Floor-strength segmentation:** Heidi/solo = fastest land (bottom-up, BYO-key, transcript+dosage floors cover the highest-frequency failures, no rich EHR needed); DAX/Epic = higher-value but procurement-gated (structured-FHIR + SNOMED floor, institutional buyer).

### (b) Clinical decision support — the OpenEvidence-dominated frontier where the floor is partial

**Tools:** OpenEvidence (44.9% — the largest single data point in the survey), Epic AI (1.8%), Perplexity (1.8%, citation-grade).

**The category splits on ONE axis: is the answer generated OVER a structured record, or as free-text synthesis?**

- **Epic AI is the honest exception — and the strongest CDS floor case by far despite its tiny 1.8% share.** The chart-summary is generated *over the patient's own FHIR record*, so the record-presence + SNOMED-subsumption floor (LIVE-proven via Hermes; T2DM is-a DM, run-`8a41ef3e`-class suppression) and FHIR structural checks apply **directly, no semantic oracle needed**. A summary that claims a Condition absent from the FHIR `Condition` list, or an allergy absent from `AllergyIntolerance`, is **deterministically falsifiable**. Defect classes: FABRICATED_HISTORY, FABRICATED_ALLERGY, MEDICATION_NOT_IN_TRANSCRIPT, WRONG_DOSAGE, INCOMPLETE_DOC. This is effectively a scribe-class SUT with a *real EHR* as ground truth.

- **OpenEvidence + Perplexity are free-text synthesis with citation pointers.** The only structured handle is the **citation scaffold**: citation-existence, guideline-currency, dosage-range, and source-authority are deterministically gradeable (KB-retrieval, Pinecone hipaa/medication-safety, rerank OFF / top-1 pinned). The wedge is a **by-construction citation-grounding corpus**: synthesize answers with INJECTED unsupported recommendations / fabricated-citation pointers (label true by construction), run the withstands-gate where a deterministic KB-retrieval check can OVERRIDE a confidently-wrong faithfulness judge on the existence axis. This is the exact o4-mini-grades-o4-mini circularity that FHIR-AgentBench documents — and where "floor-under-judge" is most validating.

**The honest gap — stated plainly because it sits under the biggest number:** the *core* of OpenEvidence's value — whether a free-text synthesized recommendation is faithfully **ENTAILED** by the cited evidence — is the **unproven semantic axis where Lithrim has NO floor.** Citation-existence is presence-checkable; citation-SUPPORT (entailment of a paraphrased claim) is not — it needs a text→concept extraction + entailment oracle Lithrim explicitly treats as **HYPOTHESIS, not shipped.** Guideline-currency has no live corpus-versioning floor today (determinism pins exist as metadata; currency-vs-latest is unbuilt). And there is **no by-construction corpus for clinical-literature QA** — the corpus was built for scribe transcripts. Even for Epic AI, two further holes: (1) the dangerous errors are *semantic-over-valid-FHIR* (un-dereferenced `MedicationRequest`→`Medication`, `Encounter.class` not filtered to inpatient) — a conformance validator alone misses these; the graph/resolution floor is **FHIR-1 (planned, MIMIC+HAPI), not built**; (2) INCOMPLETE_DOC has no presence-check floor here either. **Verdict: partial citation/scaffold floor, no faithfulness floor, no corpus.**

### (c) General AI — model providers + the ungoverned-use governance gap

**Tools:** ChatGPT (15.6%, #2 overall), Claude (3.0%), Grok (2.4%), Perplexity (1.8%) = **22.8% combined.** This is the only category where Lithrim's relationship is **INFRASTRUCTURE, not target.**

**Role 1 — MODEL PROVIDER (the live, de-risked seam):**
- **ChatGPT/OpenAI** — already shipped; the council runs on Azure-OpenAI (`_ROLE_DEPLOYMENT`), and OpenAI is the **one provider that exposes token logprobs**, making it the **calibration/ECE anchor** (`extract_verdict_confidence` returns a real `exp(logprob)`, not a self-report).
- **Claude/Anthropic** — already shipped as a first-class tool-less judge/SUT (`byo_claude_lm.py` `ClaudeCliLM`; subscription auth, no API key, $0, on-prem; run-attested, S-BS-94). **The airgapped-trust wedge** for a security-conscious health system that will not send PHI to a hosted eval.
- **Grok (xAI)** — provider seam exists (`build_judge_lm`) but **NOT registered**: "Grok as a Lithrim judge" is an **unbuilt manifest entry, not a shipped capability.** Do not overclaim.
- **Perplexity** — not a registered provider selector; see segment (b) for its citation-grade target role.

**Role 2 — UNGOVERNED CLINICAL USE (the governance opening):** when a physician pastes a transcript into ChatGPT/Claude to draft a note, patient explanation, or appeal letter, the output carries exactly Lithrim's seven defect classes with **no withstands-gate, no audit record, no governance layer.**

**Defect classes:** the structured ones embedded in a draft — FABRICATED_HISTORY, WRONG_DOSAGE, MEDICATION_NOT_IN_TRANSCRIPT, coded-condition claims — are groundable (`record_presence`, `dosage_grounding`, KB-retrieval, SNOMED-subsumption). **Grounding mechanisms apply only to those embedded structured claims.**

**The honest gap — the unifying weak point of the whole category:** the *dominant* general-AI clinical use (patient-friendly explanations, differential outlines, appeal letters, news synthesis) is **free-text prose with NO structured artifact** — no FHIR resource, no coded record, no dosage field — so `record_presence` / `dosage_grounding` / `structural_jute` **never fire and the withstands-gate collapses to judge-only.** Lithrim grounds the **structured claims embedded in a draft, not prose fidelity.** Two further losses: Anthropic **and** xAI expose **no logprobs**, so a Claude- or Grok-judged council round-trips **confidence=None** — Azure/OpenAI must stay in the roster to keep calibration measurable. Claiming a grounding floor over general-purpose free-text generation would be the **manufactured win the commercial thesis forbids.**

**The shadow-AI opening is the load-bearing market signal — and it is now a measured fact, not a hypothesis.** 67% daily use / 71% no institutional influence / 81% rollout-dissatisfaction is a textbook **broken-bounded-context**: the clinician-SME knows what "correct" means and adopts accordingly; the institution can neither keep up nor govern; the evaluation criteria live in nobody's hands. ## Open questions / where Lithrim has no floor yet

Stated ruthlessly, because the boundary *is* the product:

- **Evidence-synthesis entailment (the 44.9% core).** Citation-existence, source-authority, guideline-currency, and dosage-range are deterministically gradeable; whether a paraphrased free-text claim is **ENTAILED** by the cited passage is the **unproven semantic axis with NO floor.** It needs text→concept extraction + an entailment oracle that Lithrim treats as **HYPOTHESIS, not shipped.** Lithrim grades the citation *scaffold* deterministically and the synthesis *substance* only via the circular LLM-judge the withstands-gate is meant to replace. **There is no by-construction corpus for clinical-literature QA today.**
- **INCOMPLETE_DOC is calibration, not determinism.** You cannot presence-check the *absence* of something the model should have included against an open-ended "should" set. It fires single-judge (the diabetes over-fire was INCOMPLETE_DOC + HALLUCINATED_DETAIL, no floor). This holds for every scribe and for Epic AI alike.
- **HALLUCINATED_DETAIL on legitimate-looking prose** has no structured oracle — fired spans were genuine content. Calibration axis only.
- **Free-text prose fidelity across the general-AI lane** has **no floor at all** — no structured artifact, no by-construction labels. Lithrim's honest position there is the **model-provider seam**, not a grader of raw ChatGPT/Grok output.
- **Calibration/logprob loss for some BYO providers.** Anthropic and xAI expose **no token logprobs** → BYO-Claude / Grok judges round-trip **confidence=None**; ECE/calibration is unmeasurable on a Claude- or Grok-judged council. **Azure/OpenAI must stay in the roster as the calibration anchor.** (HYPOTHESIS: a self-consistency / sampling-based confidence proxy could partially recover this axis — unbuilt, unproven.)
- **Guideline-currency** has no live corpus-versioning floor (determinism pins are metadata; currency-vs-latest is unbuilt).
- **FHIR semantic-over-valid errors** (un-dereferenced references, `Encounter.class` filtering) need the **graph/resolution floor (FHIR-1, planned, MIMIC+HAPI)** — a conformance validator alone misses them.
- **EHR/SDK output ingestion** for Epic AI / DAX (reaching the in-EHR or enterprise-bound artifact) is roadmap (journey P4), not built.
- **Live-flip viscerality** for the scribe demo: the over-fire is context-primed (S-BS-74), so a clean single-FP live grounding-flip won't reliably reproduce. **(INFERRED** remediation: lead with the offline-twin + `8a41ef3e` + a near-miss / held-block WARN case, not a promised clean live flip.)
